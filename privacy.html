Who we are

Veltrum provides Israeli businesses with WhatsApp-based customer-communication automation — automated replies, appointment scheduling, and inquiry handling — built on the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta).

Data we process

  • Business contact details — name, phone, and email of business owners and prospects who contact us.
  • WhatsApp message content — messages sent to business WhatsApp numbers we operate for ourselves or on behalf of our business clients, including sender name, phone number, message content, and details provided for the service (e.g., a booking request).
  • Technical usage data — system logs required for reliable and secure operation.

How we use it

To provide the service (responding to inquiries, scheduling and updating appointments, alerting the relevant business owner), to operate and secure the service, and to follow up on business inquiries addressed to us. We do not sell personal data.

Sharing

Data is processed through infrastructure providers essential to the service, including Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business infrastructure) and hosting providers. When we operate a service on behalf of a business client, inquiry content is shared with that business. Beyond this, data is not disclosed to third parties unless required by law.

Retention and security

We apply reasonable, industry-standard safeguards and retain data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.
